Process

Three steps, done.

01

Export from Trimble Access

Export your job as a CSV from Trimble Access. Any standard format works — N/E/Z, E/N/Z, with or without feature codes.

02

Upload & configure

Drop the file in, set your project name, surveyor, and units. FieldCut auto-detects columns and shows every point instantly.

03

Select & print

Pick the points you need — all of them or just a few. Hit print and get one clean cut sheet per point, ready for field use.
